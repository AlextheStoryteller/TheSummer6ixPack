The tracklist panel sits at the bottom of the player and displays all six tracks on the album. Each row shows the track name on the left and its duration on the right. You can click any row at any time to load that track and start playing it immediately.

## Tracks

1. Left On Read
2. The Perfectionist
3. Sixty-Two Selfies
4. The Tourist
5. The Tourist (Reprise)
6. The Grown Up

## Active track highlight

Whichever track is currently loaded is marked as active. The active row stands out with a **white background**, **coral-colored text**, and a subtle shadow — so you can always tell at a glance which song is playing or paused.

## Track durations

Durations are populated automatically. When a track's audio file finishes loading its metadata, the player fills in the exact duration in `M:SS` format next to the track name. Until that metadata loads, the duration column shows `--:--`. In practice, durations appear quickly once you interact with a track.

<Note>
  The tracklist scrolls vertically if the panel height is exceeded. All six tracks are always accessible — just scroll down inside the list if you don't see them all at once.
</Note>
