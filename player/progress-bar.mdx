The progress bar sits between the album art and the tracklist, giving you a real-time view of where you are in the current track. It updates continuously as the song plays and lets you jump to any moment in the track with a single click.

## Reading the progress bar

The progress bar row contains three elements laid out horizontally:

- **Current time** (left) — how far into the track you are right now.
- **Progress bar** (center) — a frosted white pill that fills from left to right as the track plays. The fill uses a gradient that transitions from **gold** on the left to **coral** on the right.
- **Total duration** (right) — the full length of the loaded track.

The bar updates continuously as the track plays, keeping the fill and timestamps in sync with playback.

## Seeking

You can skip to any point in a track by clicking directly on the progress bar.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Load a track">
    Click any track in the tracklist, or use the skip buttons to navigate to the track you want.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click the bar">
    Click anywhere along the progress bar. The player calculates your click position as a fraction of the bar's total width and jumps the playback position to the corresponding moment in the song.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Playback resumes">
    The fill updates immediately to reflect the new position, and the current-time display updates to match. If the track was playing, it continues from the new position.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Seeking only works when a track is loaded and either playing or paused. Clicking the bar before you've selected any track has no effect.
</Warning>

## Time format

All times are displayed in **M:SS** format — for example, `3:42` for three minutes and forty-two seconds. There is no leading zero on the minutes.

<Note>
  Both the current time and total duration show `0:00` until a track is loaded and its audio metadata has been read. Once you select a track, the total duration populates automatically and the current time begins counting up during playback.
</Note>
