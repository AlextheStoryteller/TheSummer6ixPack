Getting the Summer 6ix Pack Player up and running takes less than a minute. There is nothing to install, no account to create, and no configuration required out of the box. Open a single file in your browser and the full player — all six tracks, all controls, the complete UI — is ready to go.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the player">
    Open `index.html` in any modern browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge). The player loads immediately — no installation or account required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a track">
    Click any track in the playlist at the bottom of the player. The track title updates at the top of the player, and the player is ready to play.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Hit Play">
    Click the **PLAY** button to start listening. The progress bar fills as the song plays, showing both elapsed time and total duration so you always know where you are in the track.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Navigate freely">
    Use the ⏮ and ⏭ buttons to skip backward or forward through the tracklist. Click anywhere on the progress bar to jump to that point in the song. When a track ends, the next one plays automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  The player works entirely in your browser — no internet connection is required once the page and audio files are loaded.
</Note>

## Browser Compatibility

The Summer 6ix Pack Player relies on standard HTML5 audio and modern CSS — no plugins needed. The following browsers are fully supported:

| Browser | Minimum Version |
|---------|----------------|
| Chrome  | 80+            |
| Firefox | 78+            |
| Safari  | 14+            |
| Edge    | 80+            |

<Tip>
  For the best audio and visual experience, keep your browser up to date. Most modern browsers update automatically, so you're likely already good to go.
</Tip>
