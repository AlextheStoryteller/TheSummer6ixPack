The Summer 6ix Pack player gives you three dedicated controls arranged in a clean, centered layout: a **Previous** skip button on the left, a large **Play/Pause** pill button in the center, and a **Next** skip button on the right. Together, these let you start, stop, and navigate the album without ever touching the tracklist.

## Controls

| Control | Button | What It Does |
|---|---|---|
| **Previous** | ⏮ (round icon) | Skips back to the previous track. From the first track, wraps around to the last. |
| **Play / Pause** | `PLAY` / `PAUSE` (pill) | Toggles playback. The label updates to reflect the current state. |
| **Next** | ⏭ (round icon) | Advances to the next track. From the last track, wraps around to the first. |

### Hover effects

The buttons respond visually when you hover over them:

- The **Play/Pause pill** scales up slightly and shifts on hover, giving it a tactile feel.
- The **Previous and Next icon buttons** gain a drop shadow on hover.

## Auto-advance

When a track finishes playing, the player automatically loads and starts the next track — no action needed on your part. After the last track ends, playback wraps back around to the first track and continues automatically.

<Tip>
  You can jump to any track instantly by clicking it in the tracklist — you don't need to use the skip buttons.
</Tip>
