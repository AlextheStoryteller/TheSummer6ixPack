The **Summer 6ix Pack Player** is a browser-based music player built for the six-track album *Summer 6ix Pack*. It features a frosted-glass glassmorphism UI, full playback controls, an interactive progress bar, and a scrollable tracklist — everything you need to experience all six tracks from start to finish. ## Quick Start

Get up and running with the Summer 6ix Pack Player in three simple steps.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Player">
    Locate the project files on your computer and open `index.html` in your preferred browser. The glassmorphism player interface will load automatically — no installation or server required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a Track">
    Click any track in the scrollable playlist to select it. The active track will be highlighted so you always know what's playing. The six tracks available are: *Left On Read*, *The Perfectionist*, *Sixty-Two Selfies*, *The Tourist*, *The Tourist (Reprise)*, and *The Grown Up*.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use the Playback Controls">
    Use the **play/pause** button to start or stop playback, the **previous** and **next** buttons to skip between tracks, and click anywhere on the **progress bar** to jump to a specific moment in the song. The player will automatically advance to the next track when one finishes.
  </Step>
</Steps>
